About Fix Social Media

Our mission to improve digital well-being

Our Mission

Social media platforms are increasingly flooded with AI-generated content, spam, and low-quality material that degrades user experience. The Fix Social Media Resource List serves as a collaborative hub for sharing and discovering tools, extensions, and resources that help users:

  • Detect and filter AI-generated content
  • Block spam and low-quality posts
  • Enhance their social media experience
  • Protect their privacy and security
  • Regain control over their digital well-being

Our Vision

We envision a future where:

  • Users have granular control over their social media consumption
  • Content filtering is based on psychological mechanisms, not just topics
  • Digital well-being tools are accessible to everyone
  • Open-source solutions empower users rather than platforms
  • Communities collaborate to build better online experiences

The Problem We're Addressing

🤖 AI-Generated Content

Social media is increasingly filled with AI-generated "slop" - low-quality, mass-produced content that adds no value.

🎣 Attention Manipulation

Platforms use sophisticated psychological techniques to capture and hold attention, often at the expense of user well-being.

📊 Algorithmic Control

Users have little control over what content they see, as algorithms optimize for engagement rather than satisfaction.

🔒 Privacy Concerns

Social media platforms collect vast amounts of data, often without users fully understanding the implications.

Our Solution

We provide a comprehensive, multi-faceted approach:

1. Curated Resource Database

A regularly updated collection of tools, browser extensions, mobile apps, and developer APIs that help users regain control over their social media experience.

2. Cognitive Awareness Framework

A groundbreaking system for identifying and moderating cognitively addictive content patterns. Unlike traditional keyword-based filters, our framework addresses the psychological mechanisms behind engagement.

3. Open Collaboration

An open-source, community-driven project where anyone can contribute resources, improvements, or feedback.

4. Educational Resources

Documentation and research to help users, developers, and policymakers understand the challenges and solutions.

Our Principles

  • User Empowerment: We believe users should control their digital experience, not algorithms
  • Open Source: We prioritize open-source solutions that are transparent and community-driven
  • Evidence-Based: Our framework and recommendations are based on research and user feedback
  • Inclusive: We welcome contributions from everyone, regardless of technical skill level
  • Privacy-Focused: We advocate for tools that respect user privacy and data sovereignty
  • Non-Commercial: This project is not-for-profit and has no commercial affiliations

Project History

The Fix Social Media Resource List was created to address the growing concern about social media quality and its impact on mental health and digital well-being. What started as a simple list of browser extensions has evolved into a comprehensive framework for understanding and addressing cognitively addictive content patterns.

The project is now maintained by a community of contributors who share a common goal: making social media better for everyone.

What Makes Us Different

Mechanism-Based Filtering

Unlike traditional content filters that rely on keywords or topics, our cognitive awareness framework identifies the psychological mechanisms used to capture attention. This allows for more nuanced and effective content moderation.

Machine-Readable Taxonomies

Our taxonomies are designed in JSON format, making them easy for developers to integrate into browser extensions, mobile apps, and other tools.

Community-Driven

We rely on community contributions to keep our resources current and expand our framework. Everyone can participate and make a difference.

Project Transfer and Growth

We welcome project transfers to the Fix Social Media organization! If you've developed a tool or resource that aligns with our mission, transferring it to our organization can provide:

  • Increased Visibility: Reach a larger audience through our community and network
  • Community Support: Access to contributors and maintainers who share your vision
  • Long-term Sustainability: Continued development and maintenance support
  • Collaborative Growth: Integration with our ecosystem of tools and resources

If you're interested in transferring your project, please start a discussion with us.

Towards a Mission-Led Foundation

As we grow and achieve impact, we are exploring the possibility of establishing Fix Social Media as a proper mission-led foundation. This transformation would enable us to:

  • Accelerate our goals with sustainable funding and resources
  • Expand our reach and impact across multiple platforms and communities
  • Support open-source projects financially and organizationally
  • Establish formal partnerships with research institutions and advocacy groups
  • Create educational programs and policy recommendations

This evolution depends on achieving meaningful traction and demonstrating real-world impact. If you believe in our mission and want to help us get there, consider contributing to the project or spreading the word about our work.
